# Decision packet

The only shape in which work may reach the owner. It goes into the card's next action with the next actor set to the owner. The nightly digest prints it; an urgent one with a deadline inside 24 hours also pages.

```
DECISION <short id> | door: disagreement | owner-only | blocker
Question: <one sentence answerable with a word or a number>
Recommendation: <A>, because <one line>.
A: <what happens>; reversible <yes | no>; cost <time, money, or risk>.
B: <what happens>; reversible <yes | no>; cost <...>.
Already done: <receipts, so the owner does not redo it>
Owning rule: <quoted verbatim, if the subject is in the filing standard's owner-override table>
If no answer by <date>: <what the team does; usually nothing, the card stays parked>
Answer here: session <name> | card <id> | PR #<n>
```

Rules:
- One question per packet. Bundling an owner-override subject with ordinary items is forbidden by the filing standard's rail 11.
- If the contract names a default, the role takes the default and reports it; it does not ask.
- Door 1 packets carry both positions and their receipts. Door 3 packets carry the exact command the owner must run, one step at a time.
